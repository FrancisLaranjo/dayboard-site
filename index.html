Dayboard

Dayboard is a personal day board for one Windows PC. It shows the day at a glance: unread Gmail, today's calendar, what is playing, the PC's state, and a few other small widgets.

It is made by one person for their own use. It is not sold, has no accounts of its own, and runs entirely on the PC it is installed on.

What it reads from Google

  • Gmail, read-only: the unread threads in the inbox and their counts, and the text of a mail when you click it to read it.
  • Google Calendar events, read-only: today's events.

Dayboard never sends, deletes, labels or marks mail as read, and never changes a calendar.
